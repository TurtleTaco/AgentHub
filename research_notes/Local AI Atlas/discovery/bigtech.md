## 1. NVIDIA–Palantir: what was announced (Oct 2025 GTC DC and later), and Palantir's own on-prem/edge offerings

### Takeaway
NVIDIA and Palantir went from an integration announcement at GTC Washington, D.C. (Oct 2025) to a full on-prem/sovereign product line within a year. Steps: (a) Palantir AIP/Ontology integrated with CUDA-X, cuOpt, Nemotron and NeMo Retriever, with Lowe's as first customer (Oct 2025). (b) A joint Sovereign AI Operating System Reference Architecture, AIOS-RA/SAIOS, on Blackwell Ultra (Mar 12, 2026). (c) Nemotron in air-gapped US agencies (Jun 2026). (d) A Dell on-prem variant (May 2026). (e) A sovereign supply-chain stack first deployed in NVIDIA's own supply chain (Sep 10, 2026), with on-prem hardware from Dell and Cisco and hosted options at Rackspace and Nebius.

### Cited Findings
**Phase 1 — GTC Washington, D.C. (Oct 28, 2025)**
- NVIDIA and Palantir announced a collaboration to integrate NVIDIA accelerated computing, CUDA-X data-processing libraries, route-optimization (cuOpt) and open models with Palantir's Ontology and AIP. The goal is an "integrated technology stack for operational AI". — [NVIDIA Investor PR](https://investor.nvidia.com/news/press-release-details/2025/Palantir-and-NVIDIA-Team-Up-to-Operationalize-AI--Turning-Enterprise-Data-Into-Dynamic-Decision-Intelligence/); [NVIDIA Newsroom](https://nvidianews.nvidia.com/news/nvidia-palantir-ai-enterprise-data-intelligence)
- NVIDIA Nemotron open reasoning models and NeMo Retriever are to be used to build AI agents informed by the Ontology. The companies are also working to bring the NVIDIA Blackwell architecture to Palantir AIP. — [NVIDIA Newsroom](https://nvidianews.nvidia.com/news/nvidia-palantir-ai-enterprise-data-intelligence); [Dataconomy](https://dataconomy.com/2025/10/30/nvidia-and-palantir-join-forces-to-build-an-ai-engine-for-enterprises)
- Lowe's is the first adopter. It is building a digital replica of its global supply chain, which was reported as roughly 7,500 vendors, 130 distribution centers and 1,700+ stores, and uses cuOpt to re-optimize shipping routes dynamically. — [NVIDIA Newsroom](https://nvidianews.nvidia.com/news/nvidia-palantir-ai-enterprise-data-intelligence); [TechInformed](https://techinformed.com/nvidia-partners-with-palantir-on-operational-ai-stack-for-enterprises/)
- At the same event, NVIDIA unveiled the **AI Factory for Government** reference design (RTX PRO Servers, HGX B200) for federal and regulated on-prem deployments. Palantir AIP is supported in it, and server makers including Dell, HPE and Cisco will offer full-stack versions. — [NVIDIA Blog](https://blogs.nvidia.com/blog/us-technology-leaders-ai-factory-design-government)

**Phase 2 — Sovereign AI Operating System Reference Architecture (Mar 12, 2026)**
- Palantir and NVIDIA announced "AIOS-RA". It is based on NVIDIA Enterprise Reference Architectures and is tested and qualified to run Palantir's full suite: AIP, Foundry, Apollo, Rubix and AIP Hub. — [BusinessWire, 2026-03-12](https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20260312795208/en/); [Constellation Research](https://www.constellationr.com/insights/news/palantir-and-nvidia-build-sovereign-premises-ai-reference-architecture)
- Hardware: NVIDIA Blackwell Ultra systems (8 GPUs per node) with Spectrum-X Ethernet. NVIDIA software: AI Enterprise, CUDA-X libraries, Nemotron open models, Magnum IO. Palantir side: a hardened Kubernetes substrate running Foundry services (Catalog, Build, Multipass), Rubix for zero-trust Kubernetes security, and Apollo for autonomous deployment and lifecycle. — [Constellation Research](https://www.constellationr.com/insights/news/palantir-and-nvidia-build-sovereign-premises-ai-reference-architecture); [Verdict](https://www.verdict.co.uk/newsletters/nvidia-palantir-unveil-joint-sovereign-ai)
- Target customers: organizations with existing GPU infrastructure, latency-sensitive workflows, data-sovereignty requirements and geographically distributed sites. Covers on-prem, edge (forward-deployed, limited connectivity) and sovereign cloud. — [Constellation Research](https://www.constellationr.com/insights/news/palantir-and-nvidia-build-sovereign-premises-ai-reference-architecture); [Stocktwits analysis](https://stocktwits.com/news-articles/markets/equity/palantir-and-nvidia-want-to-be-the-default-ai-infrastructure-for-governments/cZdD5NKRIa0)

**Phase 3 — Dell on-prem Palantir (May 18–19, 2026, Dell Technologies World)**
- Dell and Palantir announced Palantir Foundry/AIP/Ontology running on-premises inside **Dell AI Factory with NVIDIA**. It uses PowerEdge servers with NVIDIA HGX B-series accelerators, with the Ontology on Dell ObjectScale and PowerFlex storage. Apollo manages clusters with zero-trust governance, audit logging and fleet management, including air-gapped sites. Dell describes it as "coming", and no firm GA date was found. — [HPCwire (Dell release)](https://www.hpcwire.com/off-the-wire/dell-unveils-broad-ai-factory-upgrades-to-accelerate-enterprise-agentic-ai-deployment/); [Nasdaq/Motley Fool "Palantir and Dell build an AI OS for the paranoid"](https://www.nasdaq.com/articles/palantir-and-dell-build-ai-os-paranoid); [Dutch IT Channel, 2026-05-19](https://www.dutchitchannel.nl/news/734161/dell-brengt-palantir-software-naar-de-eigen-bedrijfsserver)

**Phase 4 — Nemotron in air-gapped US agencies (Jun 29, 2026)**
- An NVIDIA blog post, "Open Models, Closed Environments", describes Palantir bringing NVIDIA Nemotron open models into air-gapped US government environments on NVIDIA accelerated computing, alongside Palantir's Sovereign AI OS. — [NVIDIA Blog, 2026-06-29](https://blogs.nvidia.com/blog/2026/06/29/palantir-secure-ai-us-agencies-nemotron-open-models/)

**Phase 5 — Sovereign intelligence for supply chains (Sep 10, 2026)**
- A joint release, "NVIDIA and Palantir Bring Sovereign Intelligence to Critical Supply Chains". Palantir customers can build their own supply-chain AI stack by post-training Nemotron on proprietary data with Foundry and AIP. cuOpt handles optimization and scenario planning. — [NVIDIA Newsroom](https://nvidianews.nvidia.com/news/nvidia-and-palantir-bring-sovereign-intelligence-to-critical-supply-chains); [HPCwire repost](https://www.hpcwire.com/bigdatawire/this-just-in/nvidia-and-palantir-bring-sovereign-intelligence-to-critical-supply-chains/)
- The first deployment is NVIDIA's own supply chain. StorageReview's headline cites about 1.3 million parts in every Vera Rubin rack. — [StorageReview](https://www.storagereview.com/sv/news/palantir-and-nvidia-deploy-a-sovereign-nemotron-supply-chain-stack-starting-with-the-1-3-million-parts-in-every-vera-rubin-rack); [The Next Web](https://thenextweb.com/news/nvidia-palantir-sovereign-ai-supply-chains)
- It runs on the jointly developed Palantir Sovereign AI Operating System Reference Architecture (SAIOS). On-prem hardware comes from Dell and Cisco, and co-lo/cloud options from Rackspace and Nebius. — [The Next Web](https://thenextweb.com/news/nvidia-palantir-sovereign-ai-supply-chains); [eeNews Europe](https://www.eenewseurope.com/en/nvidia-and-palantir-bring-sovereign-ai-to-supply-chains/)

**Palantir's own on-prem/edge offerings**
- Palantir's deployable suite consists of AIP, Foundry, **Apollo** (autonomous continuous deployment across cloud, on-prem, classified and edge), **Rubix** (zero-trust Kubernetes) and AIP Hub. It is qualified on AIOS-RA for on-prem, edge and sovereign cloud. — [Constellation Research](https://www.constellationr.com/insights/news/palantir-and-nvidia-build-sovereign-premises-ai-reference-architecture)
- An earlier Oracle–Palantir tie-up covers secure cloud and AI for governments. — [DCD](https://www.datacenterdynamics.com/en/news/defense-tech-company-anduril-to-use-oracle-cloud-and-edge-offerings)

### Inferences
- Palantir is using NVIDIA as its route to becoming the default "AI OS" for sovereign and on-prem buyers. NVIDIA gains a software layer that lets OEMs (Dell, Cisco) sell turnkey sovereign stacks, and it pushes adoption of Nemotron as the open model inside closed environments.
- The arc runs from integration (2025) to a reference architecture (Q1 2026), then OEM SKUs (Q2 2026), air-gapped government use (Q2 2026) and vertical solutions (Q3 2026). That looks like a deliberate productization roadmap.

## 2. Incumbents' local-AI product lines

### Takeaway
Every major platform company now has a local-AI line. Device makers ship NPUs plus OS-level model runtimes. Infrastructure OEMs sell NVIDIA- or AMD-based "AI factories" for on-prem and sovereign use. Hyperscalers have answered sovereignty demands with disconnected or air-gapped versions of their clouds: Google GDC air-gapped Gemini, Microsoft Azure Local disconnected plus Foundry Local, Oracle Isolated regions and Roving Edge, and AWS Outposts. The 2025–26 pattern: on-device runtimes reached GA (Windows ML, Foundry Local, ExecuTorch 1.0), developer "AI supercomputer" desktops appeared (DGX Spark, Ryzen AI Halo), and frontier models were offered fully air-gapped (Gemini on GDC, large models on Foundry Local).
